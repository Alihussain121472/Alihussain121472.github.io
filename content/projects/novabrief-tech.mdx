## Overview

**NovaBrief Tech** is an AI daily briefing platform founded by Syed Ali Hussain to help students stay informed on AI breakthroughs, tech news, and academic opportunities without information overload.

## Architecture & Workflow

- **Scheduled Feed Ingestion**: Automated crawlers aggregate curated tech, AI, and student grant sources on a daily schedule.
- **Groq LPU Synthesis**: Uses Llama 3.3 on Groq LPUs for rapid, deterministic summarization and topic grouping.
- **Relational Archive & Delivery**: Stores editions and subscriber state in PostgreSQL and dispatches morning briefings via Resend and SMTP failover.
