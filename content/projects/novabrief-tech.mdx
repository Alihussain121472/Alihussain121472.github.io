## Context

**NovaBrief Tech** is a daily AI briefing for students that I founded to help university students follow AI news, software tools, and academic opportunities without reading dozens of separate feeds.

## Technical Notes

- **Automated Feed Collection**: Scheduled collectors gather tech and academic updates from curated sources each morning.
- **Consistent Summarization**: Llama 3.3 summarizes and groups stories into clear student-focused sections.
- **Email & Web Archive**: PostgreSQL stores past editions and subscriber preferences while Resend delivers the morning briefing.
