## Why Automate Daily Operations?

While building **NovaBrief Tech**, a daily AI briefing for students, repetitive tasks like social posting and answering common reader emails took time away from core development and research.

## The Two AI Agents

1. **Daily LinkedIn Posting Agent**: Connects morning briefing outputs to an LLM formatting node in n8n and schedules daily updates.
2. **Email Reply Agent**: Reads incoming messages and drafts consistent, context-aware replies for review.
