## Why Automate Daily Operations?

While building **NovaBrief Tech**, an AI daily briefing platform for students, repetitive operational tasks like social distribution and inbox triage quickly consumed engineering time.

## The Two Core Agents

1. **Daily LinkedIn Posting Agent**: Connects briefing outputs to an LLM formatting node in n8n and schedules daily updates.
2. **Email Reply Agent**: Monitors incoming messages and prepares structured, context-aware replies for rapid communication.
